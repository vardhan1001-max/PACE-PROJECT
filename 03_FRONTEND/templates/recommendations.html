How this works

Content-based recommender: for each customer, we find the 10 most behaviourally-similar customers (by segment, RFM, engagement, city tier — deliberately not by category, to avoid a circular recommendation) and suggest categories those similar customers favour. This dataset has no basket/transaction log, so this is content-based similarity, not collaborative filtering — shown here plainly rather than overclaimed.
